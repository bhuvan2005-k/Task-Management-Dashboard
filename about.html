Purpose

Why this app exists

TaskFlow is a small, focused productivity dashboard for managing work without the clutter of a generic to-do list. It keeps the overview, tasks, profile, and information pages separated so the experience feels closer to a real SaaS tool.

Workflow

How it helps

  • Shows a fast overview on the dashboard.
  • Keeps task editing and sorting inside one work area.
  • Stores user, task, and theme data in localStorage.

Features

What you get

  • Login and signup flow with a persisted session.
  • Task status, tags, overdue alerts, and toast feedback.
  • Responsive navigation and light/dark themes across pages.

Design

Why it feels realistic

  • Minimal layouts with clear hierarchy and spacing.
  • Distinct overview and work pages instead of a crowded single screen.
  • Consistent app shell and controls on every authenticated page.
